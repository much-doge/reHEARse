# ProTEFL Test Center instructions

These instructions supplement the repository-wide `AGENTS.md` for this
subproject.

Read this file, `README.md`, `docs/PRODUCT_CHARTER.md`, the active Work ID and
D-027 before editing. Keep the root reHEARse application runnable and do not
couple this application to reHEARse persistence.

## Assessment boundary

- Label test, practice and learning activities honestly; do not mix their
  result semantics.
- Formal test scores may exist only inside the Test Center assessment domain.
- Version every test form, section, timing rule, answer key and scoring rule.
- Preserve every submitted attempt and result interpretation; corrections are
  additive and auditable.
- Never infer placement from incomplete or interrupted evidence without an
  explicit, documented rule and human-visible qualification.
- Keep answer keys, protected source content and candidate responses out of
  browser payloads until authorized for the current step.
- Candidate identity, responses and results are private. Logs contain safe
  identifiers and operational status, never item text, answers or credentials.
- Use synthetic fixtures until source authorization and publication gates are
  recorded.
- Treat Zoom as an external link first. Do not request broad calendar or Zoom
  account permissions without a demonstrated need.

## Delivery language

Keep source-complete, container-verified, deployed, proctor-verified and
course-verified states distinct. A locally completed synthetic test is not a
validated placement instrument.
