Status: source-complete corrective draft

## Corrective revision

Owner review rejected the v3 draft as too terse, mechanically worded and too
dependent on source-page screenshots. The corrective revision must replace
generic phrases with natural classroom language and give every Skill 1-13 a
substantive explanation of question forms, answer location, solving procedure,
common traps and a source-faithful example. Source images remain appropriate
for passages, line references, selected Longman tables and original A-D
options, but not as a substitute for teaching the strategy.

The final corrective draft is v13. It replaces v8 as the handoff artifact.
It propagates the owner's manual cover revision, including the `WORKSHOP
ProTEFL` label, and removes visible production commentary from every guided
practice slide. Skill-level questions and answer options are now in English;
the detailed strategy instruction remains in Indonesian. Answers and rationale
remain available in speaker notes. The optional full-passage block uses
Longman Passage Four and digitizes Questions 19-28 verbatim with their original
numbering and A-D order. Question 29 is intentionally omitted so the block
contains exactly ten questions.

## Outcome

Create a concise but comprehensive 90-minute TOEFL ITP Level 1 Reading
workshop presentation based directly on the supplied Longman Reading material.
The deck covers Skills 1-13 in source order, includes guided practice and
discussion, and places an optional full-passage practice immediately before the
closing slide.
